1 · The world bible

Paste this block verbatim at the head of every prompt. It is what makes {{ n.total }} separate generations read as one place.

STYLE BIBLE — use on every image A single continuous virtual facility called the Business OS: a warm, marble-and-brass production studio built as tiered glass-walled rooms. Cinematic 3D render, physically based materials, soft volumetric daylight from a high clerestory plus warm brass practicals. Shallow depth of field. Camera is always OUTSIDE the room looking IN through a glass wall — the viewer is at the window. MATERIALS: honed cream marble floors and desks, brushed brass fittings and rails, pale oak, off-white plaster, matte charcoal steel, linen. Glass is clean, slightly tinted, with faint reflections of the viewer's side. CHARACTERS: 1–3 stylized AI workers. Smooth matte porcelain-white figures with soft featureless faces, subtly luminous seams at the joints, wearing simple linen work clothes with one brass detail. Calm, purposeful, always mid-action and looking at their work, never at the camera. Consistent height and proportion across all images. LIGHT SURFACES: work is shown on floating translucent holographic panels, physical printed sheets, and marble worktops. Panel content is abstract — blocks, rules, swatches, waveforms — never fake legible UI text. PALETTE: warm neutral base (#EDE9E3, #F7F5F1, #211E1B) with brass #C9A85C, plus ONE accent colour per image as specified in the row. TYPOGRAPHY IN IMAGE: the title from the row is set INTO the scene as physical signage — brushed brass or milled acrylic letters on the room's back wall, or etched into the glass. High-contrast, correctly spelled, elegant serif capitals. Occupies the lower-left third for station cards, the lower third centred for menu tiles. No other text anywhere in the image. No logos, no watermarks, no UI chrome. NEGATIVE: no photorealistic human faces, no text besides the specified title, no gibberish lettering, no emoji, no neon cyberpunk, no aggressive gradients, no lens flare, no cluttered desks, no floating icons, no charts with numbers.
Naming convention
station-01-brand-tree.webp
station-02-diagnostic-import.webp
menu-cockpit.webp
menu-assembly-line.webp
Lowercase, hyphenated, no spaces. Previews get a -sm suffix. Deliver as a flat folder plus a CSV of filename, title, row id.
Composition rules
Station cards: camera at standing eye height, room reads left-to-right, characters in the middle third, signage lower-left, top 30% kept quiet so card chrome can overlay. Menu tiles: one tall window, single character centred, signage lower third, deep hallway visible behind for the sense of another room beyond.
Consistency protocol
Generate station-01 first and approve it as the anchor. Feed it back as a style reference for all remaining {{ n.remaining }} so figures, marble tone and brass finish stay identical. Regenerate any image whose character proportion drifts.
