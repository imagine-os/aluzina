# A-01 - Founder dashboard

- route: `/#/founder`
- surface: founder
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/founder/specs.ts` (`homeSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

Alejandra Guerra's landing page. It answers four questions in one screen: what waits for her decision, where every project sits in the pipeline, what is on the calendar this week, and how the money looks. Nothing here belongs to another role: the alerts and the payments are read only, with a line saying Miguel owns them.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (A-01) with a primary "See all approvals" button.
2. Four `StatTile`s: waiting for you, live projects, open alerts (urgent count as hint), owed to the studio (overdue count as hint). The first two navigate.
3. Two columns: Card "Waiting for you" with an `ApprovalQueue` of the first three projects; Card "Pipeline by phase" with one compact `StatTile` per phase, each opening A-03.
4. Two columns: Card "This week" (`DataTable` of meetings starting in the next seven days, with a relative day) and Card "Alerts from operations" (`DataTable` of the six most severe open alerts).
5. Card "Money": `KeyValue` with owed to the studio, owed to suppliers, overdue payment count and the budget carried by live projects.

## Data

Reads `projects`, `consistencyChecks`, `alerts`, `meetings`, `payments`. Writes (through the shared approval hook) `projects.approval`, `alerts.status` and new `tasks` rows.

## Rules

None numbered yet; the approval chain follows `docs/knowledge/roles-and-portals.md` ("Approval chain").

## Logic

- The approvals card calls `useApprovals()`, the same hook A-02 uses, so a decision taken here writes exactly the same rows and both pages re-render live.
- Money: owed = `amountCop - paidCop` over unpaid payments, split by direction; the budget total only counts projects not yet delivered.
- Meetings are filtered with `daysUntil(startsAt)` between 0 and 7 and labelled today / tomorrow / in n days.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |

| `founder.openApprovals` | Review approvals | open what is waiting for my approval | `projects.approve` | — |

| `founder.commentApproval` | Comment on an approval | leave the note {note} on the project {project} | `projects.approve` | `project: id`, `note: string` |
