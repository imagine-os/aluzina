# O-09 - Alerts before urgent

- route: `/#/ops/alerts`
- surface: ops
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/ops/specs.ts` (`alertsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

"Alerting the founder before a situation becomes urgent". Every alert is a dated item with a lead time and a pointer at the thing it is about — a quote that expires, a payment that is due, a project waiting for a decision. Miguel works the list from open to resolved.

## Screenshots

`docs/screenshots/O-09/en-390.jpg`, `en-1280.jpg` — captured by the integrator.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (O-09) with a `Placeholder` "New alert" button.
2. `Tabs`: open | acknowledged | resolved, each with its count.
3. One `Card` per alert (raised when urgent): severity and status pills, the lead-time badge, what it is about, whose role it belongs to; the footer carries Acknowledge / Resolve / Reopen and "Open the item".
4. `EmptyState` when a tab is empty.

## Data

Reads `alerts`, `quotes`, `payments`, `deliveries`, `projects`. Writes `alerts.status`.

## Rules

None numbered yet; this is the shared alert pattern named in `docs/knowledge/roles-and-portals.md`.

## Logic

- Acknowledge, resolve and reopen write `alerts.status` and confirm with a toast.
- The subtitle says "due today" / "in n days" / "n days overdue"; the lead badge is tinted by the same date compared with `leadDays` (inside the window = warning, past = danger).
- "About" resolves `entity` + `entityId` to a human title: the quote item, the payment counterparty and concept, the delivery item with its supplier, or the project name.
- "Open the item" navigates to the ops page that owns that entity.
- Alerts for other roles are listed too, labelled with the role, because Miguel is the one who chases them.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ops.acknowledgeAlert` | Acknowledge alert | acknowledge the alert {alert} | `alerts.manage` | `alert: id` |
| `ops.resolveAlert` | Resolve alert | resolve the alert {alert} | `alerts.manage` | `alert: id` |
| `ops.reopenAlert` | Reopen alert | reopen the alert {alert} | `alerts.manage` | `alert: id` |
| `ops.openAlertSubject` | Open the item | open the page of the item behind the alert {alert} | `projects.read` | `alert: id` |
| `ops.newAlert` | New alert | create an alert about {subject} for {date} | `alerts.manage` | `subject: string`, `date: date` |

## Real vs mock / placeholder

The three status writes and the navigation are real. `Placeholder`: "New alert" — creating one needs a form and, better, a rule engine that raises alerts from due dates automatically.

## Responsive and input check (P-01, P-03)

Checked at 360 / 390 / 768 / 1280 / 1920, light and dark. Cards are an `auto-fill` grid at 26rem; footers wrap so the buttons stay 44 px on a 360 px phone.
