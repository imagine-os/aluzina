The key being that each system's point of view can see into the hoy hub contents in its own way." ## Rules

## Logic

- One `useHubMap` feeds three `buildLens()` calls with the lens hint's `showTools`; each small desk is its own `useDesk` (code `D-16-<lens>`, height stored per lens) with `actions: false`, so the three do not fight over the `desk.*` ids; the page registers `clienthub.openLens`, `clienthub.setFacesLang`, `clienthub.reloadMap`.
- An object or a figure opens `/founder/clients/hoy/hub?lens=<lens>&faces=<lang>&open=<id>` (W-05 opens that object's drawer).
