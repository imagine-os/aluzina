# O-05 - Quotes and comparisons

- route: `/#/ops/quotes`
- surface: ops
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/ops/specs.ts` (`quotesSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

"Requesting and organizing price quotes" and "preparing price comparisons": one card per comparison group with its quotes side by side, the best price and the shortest lead time marked, the spread stated, and one winner selectable — which is the decision the founder then sees in her own portal.

## Screenshots

`docs/screenshots/O-05/en-390.jpg`, `en-1280.jpg` — captured by the integrator.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (O-05) with a `Placeholder` "Request quote" button.
2. `FilterBar`: search (item, group, supplier), project select, count, clear.
3. One `Card` per `comparisonGroup`: title is the group, subtitle is the project plus the spread (or the item when the group has a single quote), body is a `DataTable` of the quotes with Select and Shortlist row actions.
4. `Drawer`: the quote in full, including the notes the supplier gave, with Select.

## Data

Reads `quotes`, `suppliers`, `projects`. Writes `quotes.status`.

## Rules

None numbered yet. The quote chain (Miguel compares, the founder writes the client quote) is in `docs/knowledge/roles-and-portals.md`.

## Logic

- Select writes `status = 'selected'` on the chosen quote and `status = 'rejected'` on every other quote of the same group, one write per row through `useData`.
- Shortlist writes `status = 'shortlisted'` on that quote only, and is offered for `requested` and `received` quotes.
- "Best price" and "Fastest" are computed per group from the live rows; the spread is the highest price minus the lowest.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ops.selectQuote` | Select quote | select the quote {quote} | `quotes.compare` | `quote: id` |
| `ops.shortlistQuote` | Shortlist quote | shortlist the quote {quote} | `quotes.compare` | `quote: id` |
| `ops.viewQuote` | Open quote | show the quote {quote} | `quotes.compare` | `quote: id` |
| `ops.requestQuote` | Request quote | request a quote from {supplier} for {item} | `quotes.request` | `supplier: id`, `item: string` |
