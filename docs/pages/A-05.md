# A-05 - Products and partnerships

- route: `/#/founder/products`
- surface: founder
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/founder/specs.ts` (`productsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

Lighting fixture and product development, plus the partnerships that make it possible. One card per product project (today: Honey Valley Lighting) with its materials, its render and supplier packs and its open tasks, and below them the strategic meetings where a partnership or a major negotiation happens.

## Screenshots

`docs/screenshots/A-05/en-390.jpg`, `en-1280.jpg` — captured by the integrator.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (A-05) with a "New partnership" `Placeholder`.
2. One `Card` per project of type `lighting-product`: a `KeyValue` header (phase, creative direction, budget, location), then three dense tables — materials (with Approve), render / supplier packs, open tasks.
3. Card "Strategic meetings and negotiations": the `strategic` meetings, soonest first.
4. Card "Collection roadmap": a `Placeholder` explaining that prototype, tooling and launch dates need a product entity.

## Data

Reads `projects` (filtered to `lighting-product`), `materials`, `renderPacks`, `tasks`, `meetings` (kind `strategic`). Writes `materials.status`.

## Rules

None numbered.

## Logic

- Product projects are selected by `type`, not by name, so a second collection appears without a code change.
- Approve material writes `status: 'approved'` under `products.write`; it is only offered on materials that are not already approved. This crosses into `materials.manage` territory (Sarai's) and is listed as a decision to confirm.
- Render packs and tasks are read only here; S-07 and O-03 own them.
- When no project has type `lighting-product`, an `EmptyState` explains what would appear.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `founder.approveMaterial` | Approve material | approve the material {material} for the collection | `products.write` | `material: id` |
| `founder.newPartnership` | New partnership | record a new partnership with {partner} | `partnerships.write` | `partner: string` |

## Components

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Real: the product cards, the three tables, approving a material, the strategic meetings. `Placeholder`: "New partnership" (no partnerships entity) and "Collection roadmap" (no products entity).
