# A-06 - Clients and negotiations

- route: `/#/founder/clients`
- surface: founder
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/founder/specs.ts` (`clientsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

Client relations, sales and major negotiations in one table. Every client with the projects they own, the budget in play, what they still owe the studio and the next conversation booked, so the founder can prepare a meeting in one look and open the full picture in a drawer.

## Screenshots

`docs/screenshots/A-06/en-390.jpg`, `en-1280.jpg` — captured by the integrator.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (A-06) with a "New lead" `Placeholder`.
2. Three `StatTile`s: clients, budget in play, owed to the studio.
3. `FilterBar` with a `SearchField` over client and project names.
4. `DataTable` of clients (client, projects, budget, outstanding, next meeting), sorted by budget, row opens the drawer.
5. `Drawer`: a `KeyValue` summary and four dense tables — projects (phase, approval), payments, meetings, documents — with a "Log a note" `Placeholder` in the footer.

## Data

Reads `projects`, `payments`, `meetings`, `documents`. Writes nothing.

## Rules

None numbered.

## Logic

- There is no `clients` entity: rows are derived from distinct `projects.client` values, which is why the page is read only.
- Outstanding = sum of `amountCop - paidCop` over `direction: 'in'` payments that are not paid, matched either by the client's project ids or by `counterparty`.
- Next meeting = the first meeting of any of that client's projects with `daysUntil >= 0`.
