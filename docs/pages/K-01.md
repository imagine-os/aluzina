## Purpose

The Hub's own organizer replacing the Slack sidebar (prompt 0005, D-026, D-027): a tree of spaces without depth limit on the left, the posts filed in the selected space on the right. A post is one row shown in every space it is filed in, so what applies to several roles is filed once. Entering as a role preselects that role's space.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (Spaces; breadcrumb Portal > Spaces > ancestors; actions: Browse spaces (under 1024 px), Archive / Restore (spaces.admin, on K-02), New space, New post (spaces.write)).
2. Role banner (`Card` raised): "For your role: <space>", count of posts filed to the role, "Go to my role space" when another space is selected.
3. Two columns: left `SearchField` + "Show archived" `Checkbox` + `SpaceTree`; right the selected space: heading with glyph and kind pill, description (`Markdown`, "Edit description" -> `Textarea` + Save / Cancel), child spaces grid (`Card` buttons with post counts), `FilterBar` (kind, tag, author, sort; count summary), `PostCard` list pinned first.
4. `Drawer` (left) with the same tree under 1024 px; `Modal`s New space (name, kind, parent, description) and New post (title, kind, URL for links, Markdown body, tags).

## Data

Reads `spaces`, `posts`, `filings`, `tags`. Writes `spaces` (create, description, archived), `posts` (create), `filings` (create with the new post).

## Rules

None numbered. `spaces.read` guards the route; `spaces.write` shows the editors; `spaces.admin` shows Archive. Draft / archived posts are visible to their author and to writers only. A post can not be unfiled from its last space (K-03).

## Logic

- Selection: route `:spaceId` (K-02) > the space about my role (`aboutType = roles`) > the first area. The path to the selection is expanded on change; search expands the tree around matches (spaces and their ancestors) and switches the list to matching posts across all spaces.
- Pinned first, then sort (updated, title, kind, author); filters by kind, tag (registry + used tags) and author; "also in N spaces" = other filings of the post.
- Writes carry `basedOn = space.updated_at` (D-024) and reach other tabs through the provider (D-023).
- Keyboard: the tree follows the WAI-ARIA tree pattern (ArrowUp / Down move, ArrowRight expands or enters, ArrowLeft collapses or goes to the parent, Home / End, Enter / Space select, a letter jumps, `*` expands the level); every card is one button.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `spaces.selectSpace` | Open space | open the space {space} | `spaces.read` | `space: id` |
| `spaces.toggleNode` | Expand or collapse | expand the space {space} in the tree | `spaces.read` | `space: id` |
| `spaces.search` | Search spaces and posts | search spaces and posts for {query} | `spaces.read` | `query: string` |
| `spaces.showArchived` | Show archived | show or hide archived spaces | `spaces.read` | – |
| `spaces.browseTree` | Browse spaces | open the spaces tree | `spaces.read` | – |
| `spaces.createSpace` | New space | create a space called {name} under {parent} | `spaces.write` | `name, kind, parent` |
| `spaces.createPost` | New post | write a {kind} called {title} in {space} | `spaces.write` | `title, kind, space` |
| `spaces.openPost` | Open post | open the post {post} | `spaces.read` | `post: id` |
| `spaces.filterKind` / `filterTag` / `filterAuthor` | Filter | show only {kind} / tagged {tag} / by {person} | `spaces.read` | enum / string / id |
| `spaces.sort` | Sort posts | sort the posts by {by} | `spaces.read` | `by: enum:updated\|title\|kind\|author` |
| `spaces.editDescription` | Edit description | describe the space {space}: {text} | `spaces.write` | `space, text` |
| `spaces.archive` | Archive space | archive the space {space} | `spaces.admin` | `space: id` |
| `spaces.goToRole` | Go to my role space | open the space of my role | `spaces.read` | – |

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Everything writes real rows through the DataProvider (mock, localStorage, cross-tab). No `Placeholder`. Not built: move / reorder spaces, visibility enforcement beyond the field, post versions.
