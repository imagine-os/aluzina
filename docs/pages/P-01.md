# P-01 - Services

- route: `/#/services`
- surface: public (bare shell, no permission)
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/public/specs.ts`
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

The public front door of Aluzina inside the OS (D-035). A visitor in Medellín who found the studio on Instagram, through a referral or on aluzinaa.com lands here and learns who the studio is (interior design and emotional lighting; Alejandra Guerra as Founder & Creative Director), which of the five services fits their space, how a project unfolds, and how to start one. The page never mentions internal vocabulary: no service codes, no pipeline statuses, no phase ids.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PublicLayout` header: wordmark "Aluzina" + tagline "Spaces + Light + Experiences that transform", section nav read from the route manifest (Services, Start your project, Our method), `GlobalControls` (EN/ES, theme, dev mode), "Staff: open the Hub" link to `#/`.
2. Hero: eyebrow tagline, h1 "Interior design and emotional lighting in Medellín", lead paragraph, founder line, CTAs "Start your project" (primary) and "Read our method".
3. **Five ways to work with us**: one `Card` per `SERVICES` entry - ladder word `Badge` on its own line, service name, outcome as subtitle, "Ideal for", "The question it answers" (or "Our promise" for Execution, which has no central question), and a "See this service" button to `/services/:slug`.
4. **How we work**: the 10-step `CLIENT_JOURNEY` as a numbered grid (01 Lead … 10 Follow-up).
5. **One studio. One method. Different depths of service.**: the `SERVICE_LADDER_LOGIC` paragraph ("A client can enter ALUZINA through a focused service…").
6. Closing CTA card "Tell us about your space" -> `/start`.
7. `PublicLayout` footer: studio line, `aluzinaa.com`, Instagram and WhatsApp (`Placeholder`s), contact hint.

## Data

None. Everything is rendered from `src/domain/playbook.ts` (`SERVICES`, `CLIENT_JOURNEY`, `SERVICE_LADDER_LOGIC`) through `pick(text, lang)`.

## Rules

G-10 in spirit: the page presents the ladder without pushing the deepest service; the routing decision happens in P-03 and is always framed as a suggestion.

## Logic

- No permission, no session requirement: the route has no `permission` and uses the `bare` shell, so the module draws its own chrome in `PublicLayout`.
- The header nav is derived from the route manifest (`useRoutes()` filtered to `surface === 'public'` with a `nav` entry, sorted by `nav.order`), so a new public page appears in the nav by existing.
- Card order follows `SERVICES` (01, 02, 03, E, 04), the playbook's own ladder order.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `public.openService` | Open a service | open the page of the service {service} | – | `service: enum:<slug>` |
| `public.startProject` | Start a project | start the intake form, optionally for the service {service} | – | `service: enum:01\|02\|03\|E\|04` |
| `public.openWebsite` | Open the studio site | open the Aluzina website aluzinaa.com | – | – |
| `public.openHub` | Open the staff hub | open the Aluzina Business OS hub | – | – |

All four register while mounted (`useRegisterActions`), so `window.__aluzina.actions.run('public.openService', { service: 'interior-styling' })` navigates.

## Components

`Card`, `Badge`, `Button`, `Placeholder`, `ToggleButton` (through `GlobalControls`).

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Real: everything on the page - the services, the journey, the ladder logic, the navigation, the language and theme toggles. Placeholder: Instagram and WhatsApp in the footer (the studio has not given handles or a number; 2 `data-placeholder` elements, shared with every public page).

## Responsive and input check (P-01, P-03)

Verified with Playwright at 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560, 3840: no horizontal overflow at any width, in EN and ES, light and dark. Every focusable is >= 44 x 44 px (measured: 20 focusables at 390, none under 44 px). Focus order: skip link, brand, the three nav links, EN/ES, theme, dev, staff hub, hero CTAs, then the cards in reading order. Nothing hover-only; the ladder word and the "See this service" button are always visible. Grid items carry `min-width: 0` so the page never scrolls sideways at 360.

## Strings (P-13)

Namespace `public.*`. EN complete, ES complete (the public site speaks to Spanish-speaking clients, so no key falls back to English). Service copy comes from `domain/playbook.ts`, where a few Spanish strings are the integrator's translation awaiting the founder's review (D-033).
