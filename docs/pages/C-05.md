# C-05 - Payments (client)

- route: `/#/client/payments`
- surface: client (PhoneShell)
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/client/specs.ts` (`paymentsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

What the client has been billed, what they have paid and what is still open — read only, in COP, with the overdue count called out. Paying online is deliberately not wired: Stripe comes later (D-035), so the button is a `Placeholder` instead of a lie.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (C-05).
2. Three `StatTile`s: billed, paid, outstanding (with "1 overdue" as the hint and a danger tone when something is overdue).
3. `Card` with the `DataTable` of my payments: concept, project, due date, amount, outstanding, status pill; sortable, stacking into cards under 768 px.
4. The `Placeholder` "Pay online" button and the read-only note.

## Data

Reads `projects` (scoped), `payments`. Writes nothing.

## Rules

D-035 (payment steps stay `Placeholder` until Stripe). Money is owned by operations (O-07); the client app never writes it.

## Logic

- Only payments whose `projectId` is one of mine **and** whose `direction` is `in`. What the studio owes its suppliers is never shown to the client — that is the rule this page enforces.
- Totals are summed from `amountCop` / `paidCop` and rendered with `formatCop` in the current language (`$ 18.500.000` in ES, `COP 18,500,000` in EN).
- `client.payOnline` is registered anyway, so voice and D-09 get the honest answer "not wired yet: online payment arrives with Stripe (D-035)" instead of `not-live`.

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Real: the list, the filtering, the totals and the statuses. `Placeholder`: "Pay online" (tooltip on hover and focus, toast on activation, dashed badge in dev mode — verified). Data is the seeded `pay-laureles-anticipo` (COP 18,500,000, overdue).

## Responsive and input check (P-01, P-03)

Verified at 360 / 390 / 768 / 1280 / 1920, light and dark. The table stacks into labelled cards on a phone; the amount columns are end-aligned and never wrap a COP figure.

## Strings (P-13)

Namespace `client.payments.*`. EN complete, ES complete.
