# P-03 - Start your project

- route: `/#/start` (accepts `?service=<code>`)
- surface: public (bare shell, no permission)
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/public/specs.ts`
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

The public intake: the "purchase flow" of the standard deliverable batch (P-12) and the first step of the playbook's commercial process ("every inquiry must enter a single traceable pipeline"). A visitor answers four short steps, sees the path the studio suggests in plain language, consents to being contacted, and sends the request. The result is one `leads` row in the pipeline, with a reference number the visitor can quote.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PublicLayout` header.
2. `PageHeader`: "Start your project" + subtitle.
3. Stepper: four steps; visited steps are `Button`s that jump back, the current one carries `aria-current="step"`, future ones are dashed chips. Progress text "Step 2 of 4".
4. Step 1 **About you**: name*, email*, phone (optional), city*.
5. Step 2 **Your space**: project type* (residential / commercial / hospitality / wellness / other), approximate m², current state (built / under construction / conceptual), is there a floor plan (yes / no).
6. Step 3 **What you need**: the remaining qualification questions - what do you want to transform*, why now, how far do you want to take it* (ideas / full design / styling), would you like Aluzina to carry out the work (yes / no / maybe later), investment range, when to start - plus how did you find Aluzina* (7 `LEAD_CHANNELS`) and the service they already have in mind (preselected from `?service=`).
7. Step 4 **Review and send**: `KeyValue` summary of every answer, the suggested path (`routeService()`) with its ladder word, name, outcome and reason, the "and afterwards" line when the route has a follow-on service, the "our team confirms the right path with you" sentence, consent checkbox*, Submit.
8. Confirmation screen: reference number (`lead.id`), what happens now (three steps), the suggested service, and then either the **Reserve with a deposit** `Placeholder` (paths 01 / 02 / 04) or the "a proposal follows the consultation" note (paths 03 / E).
9. `PublicLayout` footer.

## Data

Writes one `leads` row through `useData().create('leads', …)`:

`name`, `phone | null`, `email`, `city`, `projectType` (from the typology answer, `other` when unset), `areaM2 | null`, `projectStatus | null`, `requestedService` (form or `?service=`), `suggestedService` (from `routeService()`), `budgetCop | null`, `desiredStart | null`, `channel`, `ownerId: null`, `status: 'lead-new'`, `qualification` (all ten playbook keys that were answered), `notes: ''`, `projectId: null`, `source: 'public-intake'`.

Reads nothing.

## Rules

- **G-10**: `routeService()` only suggests. The review step says the suggestion is a first read and that the team confirms the path; the founder routes in the CRM.
- **D-035**: the payment step is a `Placeholder` until Stripe.

## Logic

- **Persistence**: `{ step, values }` in `sessionStorage` under `aluzina.public.intake`, written on every change, read on mount, removed once the lead is created. A reload, a language switch or a wrong Back never costs the visitor their answers (verified).
- **Validation** runs per step on Next and on Submit: required are name, a syntactically valid email, city, project type, what they want to transform, the depth they need, the channel and the consent box; the area must parse to a positive number when filled. Errors render inline through the field components (`role="alert"`), a summary line above the form is announced, and focus moves to the first invalid control.
- **One answer map**: steps 2 and 3 both write into the `QUALIFICATION_QUESTIONS` key space (`typology`, `areaM2`, `floorPlan`, `projectStatus`, `transform`, `why`, `depth`, `execute`, `investment`, `start`), so `routeService()` sees every answer and `leads.qualification` stores the playbook's own keys.
- **Budget**: the investment answer is free text. It becomes `budgetCop` only when it carries exactly one amount of at least six digits ("120.000.000" -> 120000000); a range ("between 120 and 150 million") stays `null` and the sentence lives in `qualification.investment`. An unknown fact is never guessed.
- **Desired start**: only an ISO date (`2027-01-15`) lands in `leads.desiredStart`; anything else stays in `qualification.start`.
- **Confirmation**: the reference is the row id (e.g. `lea-muarxg3d2`). The next-steps text says "the studio will contact you to schedule the diagnosis" - no response-time promise is made, because the playbook does not state one.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `public.nextStep` | Next step | go to the next step of the intake form | – | – |
| `public.prevStep` | Previous step | go back to the previous step of the intake form | – | – |
| `public.submitIntake` | Send the request | send the intake form and open a lead | – | – |
| `public.reserveDeposit` | Reserve with a deposit | pay the deposit that reserves the service {service} | – | `service: enum:01\|02\|03\|E\|04` |
| `public.startProject` | Start a project | start the intake form, optionally for the service {service} | – | `service: enum:01\|02\|03\|E\|04` |
| `public.openWebsite` | Open the studio site | open the Aluzina website aluzinaa.com | – | – |
| `public.openHub` | Open the staff hub | open the Aluzina Business OS hub | – | – |

Registration follows the visible UI: `public.nextStep` only while a next step exists, `public.prevStep` only from step 2, `public.submitIntake` only on step 4, `public.reserveDeposit` only on a confirmation screen that shows the deposit button. `public.reserveDeposit` is registered but honest: it returns `"not wired yet: online deposits arrive with the payments integration (D-035)"` instead of pretending to charge (verified through `window.__aluzina.actions.run`).

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Real: the whole flow, the validation, the persistence, the routing suggestion and the `leads` write (verified end to end: a submitted form produced a `lead-new` / `public-intake` row with the full qualification map, and the confirmation showed its id). ## Responsive and input check (P-01, P-03)

## Strings (P-13)

Namespace `public.start.*`. EN complete, ES complete. The qualification questions are re-voiced in the second person for the visitor (`public.start.q.<key>`) while the keys, the option values and the option labels stay the playbook's (`QUALIFICATION_QUESTIONS`), so the CRM and the form never drift apart.

## Open questions

- **Anonymous writes**: `useData().create` is unguarded today. The Supabase adapter needs a row-level policy that lets an anonymous visitor insert one `leads` row with `source = 'public-intake'`, `status = 'lead-new'` and `ownerId = null`, and read nothing back. Rate limiting / spam protection is not designed yet.
- Should the form ask for photos of the space (services 01 and 02 both start with a digital survey)? That needs file storage.
- Confirmation email / WhatsApp to the visitor: no messaging provider yet.
- Response-time promise ("we reply within N business days") is not in the playbook; the founder should set one.
