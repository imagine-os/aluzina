# S-09 - Measurements and requirements

- route: `/#/studio/measurements`
- surface: studio
- status: built (placeholder-heavy)
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/studio/specs.ts` (`measurementsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

"Verifying measurements and requirements" (the founder's roster). The page shows which sites still have to be verified before documentation, which site visits are open, and how many schedules are waiting on them. The capture tools - dimension sheet, requirements checklist, site photos - have no entity yet and are marked as such.

## Screenshots

Not captured in this pass; working captures at 390 and 1280 in the worker scratchpad.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` with a `Placeholder` "Open measurement sheet".
2. Three `StatTile`s: projects to verify, open site tasks, schedules waiting.
3. `Card` "Projects and sites": `DataTable` (project, location, phase, due).
4. `Card` "Site visits and measurement tasks": read-only `DataTable` (title, project, assignee, due, status).
5. Three `Placeholder` `Card`s: room-by-room dimensions, requirements per space, site photos and annotations - each with a sentence describing what it will hold.

## Data

Reads `projects`, `tasks` (where `ownerRole = studio`), `schedules`. Writes nothing.

## Rules

Task status belongs to operations (`tasks.manage`), so nothing here writes to a task.

## Logic

- Projects to verify = phase `development` or `documentation`; open site tasks = studio tasks not `done`; schedules waiting = schedules not `final`.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `studio.openMeasurementSheet` | Open measurement sheet | open the measurement sheet of {project} | `measurements.write` | `project: id` |
| `studio.captureDimensions` | Room-by-room dimensions | capture the dimensions of {space} in {project} | `measurements.write` | `project: id`, `space: string` |
| `studio.addRequirement` | Requirements per space | record a requirement for {space} in {project} | `measurements.write` | `project: id`, `space: string` |
| `studio.attachSitePhoto` | Site photos and annotations | attach a site photo to {project} | `measurements.write` | `project: id` |
