# A-07 - Team overview

- route: `/#/founder/team`
- surface: founder
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/founder/specs.ts` (`teamSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

What each of the four roles is carrying right now. A tab per person over their task list, the open alerts with the role they belong to, the meetings everyone shares, and the founder's own list — the only one she can tick off from here.

## Screenshots

`docs/screenshots/A-07/en-390.jpg`, `en-1280.jpg` — captured by the integrator.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (A-07).
2. Four `StatTile`s, one per role, showing open task counts; activating one switches the tab.
3. Card "Tasks by role": a "Show tasks already done" `Checkbox` in the card actions and `Tabs` (founder, ops, studio, brand) over a `DataTable` (title, project, owner, priority, due, status).
4. Two columns: Card "Alerts" (`DataTable` of open alerts with the role they are for) and Card "Shared meetings" (internal and strategic meetings).
5. Card "Vision and growth": a `Placeholder` for goals.

## Data

Reads `tasks`, `alerts`, `meetings`, `projects`. Writes `tasks.status` (founder-owned tasks only).

## Rules

None numbered.

## Logic

- Tasks are grouped by `ownerRole`; done tasks are hidden until the checkbox is on.
- "Mark done" is only offered when `ownerRole === 'founder'` and the viewer has `projects.write`; the other roles keep their own boards (O-03, S-xx, G-xx).
- Alerts are read only: `alerts.manage` is Miguel's (O-09), so the card carries the same "Miguel owns these" note as A-01.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `founder.completeTask` | Mark my task done | mark my task {task} as done | `projects.write` | `task: id` |
| `founder.showDoneTasks` | Show done tasks | show the tasks that are already done | `projects.read` | `on: boolean` |
| `founder.openGoals` | Open vision and growth | open the vision and growth goals | `projects.read` | — |

## Components

`PageHeader`, `StatTile`, `Tabs`, `DataTable`, `Checkbox`, `Card`, `StatusPill`, `Badge`, `Placeholder`.

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Real: the role tiles, the tabs, the task table, marking a founder task done, the alerts and meetings tables. `Placeholder`: "Vision and growth" (no goals entity).
