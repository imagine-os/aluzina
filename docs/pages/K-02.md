# K-02 - Space

- route: `/#/<surface>/spaces/:spaceId` (founder, ops, studio, brand, dev)

## Purpose

One space deep-linked: its ancestors as breadcrumb, its description, the spaces inside it and the posts filed in it. It is the K-01 page with the selection taken from the route, so every space has a stable address (P-06) that a post's "Filed in" chips, the graph, the catalogs and K-06 link to.
