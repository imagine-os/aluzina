## Purpose

The whole method at a glance, as physical objects on a desk (Justin, prompt 0026: "a very simple beautiful visual page ... every template and item needs to have a preview ... a zoomable canvas ... as if they're physical objects ... each phase can have a mat, and each mat can have different mats on top"). One felt mat per phase of the client journey; on each mat, lighter sub-mats group the phase's objects; every status, form, checklist, template phase, deliverable, rule, role and KPI of the playbook and the project template is a small sheet, form, checklist, document, folder, box, token or card on 64 px chess squares, and its top face is a miniature of its real content. Anyone on a portal can zoom out to see the shape of the method, or zoom in and read any object. On the near edge of every mat a seated professional at a small desk stands for the role that owns the phase (prompt 0027: "nice character models, professional characters at a desk for each mat", D-104).

Since changelog 0035 (prompt 0028, D-105) the desk is also the **spatial view of one project**: follow a project and its status glows on its phase's mat, every record of the project (leads, services, meetings, documents, quotes, change orders, revisions, purchases, payments, deliveries, site reports, messages, alerts) lands as a small tile of light on the mat where that kind of record belongs, a money and communication rail sums what was quoted, approved, paid and is outstanding, and the project's events travel as light from mat to mat ("it is all light: a message, a payment, a status change are one kind of event moving through phases"). Every Open on the page navigates.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (W-04): breadcrumb portal / Method desk, title, subtitle with the object, people and mat counts (the subtitle is hidden under 480 px; the hint under the stage says the same).
2. Toolbar (`role="toolbar"`, wraps on phones): zoom − / percentage (`aria-live`) / zoom +, Fit, Tilt toggle (`aria-pressed`, "Tilted" / "Flat"), phase `Select` ("Go to a phase…", `NN Phase · count`), Reset.
2b. Light toolbar (`role="toolbar"`, wraps): project `Select` ("Follow a project…" / "Seguir un proyecto…", `Name · status`, active projects first, then prospects, then past, each by `updated_at`), and when a project is followed: Clear, Previous (‹), Play / Pause (`aria-pressed`), Next (›). Under it, with a project followed, the trail caption line (`aria-live="polite"`): "Event 7 of 21 · Aug 29, 2026 · 08 Delivery: Site report, 55 %: …", or the idle line with the tile and event counts.
3. The stage: the rest of the viewport height (40 dvh minimum on phones, the bottom nav reserved). Inside: `perspective` > camera (`rotateX(22deg)` or flat) > world (translate + gesture scale) > zoom layer (CSS `zoom` = committed zoom) > desk slab (wood) > ten mats.
4. Mat: label button (number, phase name, object count) + sub-mats (label strip, chess-square checker) + objects, then the person's station on the near edge (5 x 3 squares, centred, below the sub-mats): rug, desk, chair, seated figure, props, tent nameplate.
4b. With a project followed: its **status token** has a breathing halo; its phase's **mat** wears concentric rings of the brand light (blue, aqua, mint, lime) and a glow; earlier mats are tinted mint ("done"); each mat with records of the project gets a sub-mat labelled with the project's short name (and "<name> · messages" for messages) after its playbook sub-mats, holding **light tiles** (1 square each); a **pulse** (a dot of light, 28 screen px) sits on the trail's current stop.
4c. **Money and communication rail** (with a project followed), attached to the stage's near edge, outside the mats (rem, UI chrome): Following (name, status · phase), Quoted, Approved, Paid (in / out), Outstanding (in / out), Communication (messages, client / team, meetings, lead channel). Six columns from 1100 px, three from 600, two below; the stage height subtracts it so the page still ends at the viewport on desktop.
5. Hint line (how to zoom, pan, use the keys, follow a project, play the trail).
6. `Drawer` (right from 768 px, bottom sheet below): for an object, large preview, `KeyValue` (kind, phase, sub-mat, source, code, id), the full contents list, and **Open** (navigates); for a person, a large flat portrait, `KeyValue` (playbook role, portal role, demo person, phase, basis), why here, responsibilities, the phases this role owns on the desk, and **Open portal** (navigates); for a light tile, its face large, `KeyValue` (kind of record, project, phase, managed on), why this mat (the rule's rationale), every field of the row (`KeyValue`, labelled EN / ES, amounts `formatCop`, dates `formatDate`, suppliers and people by name, live), and **Open** to the page that manages the entity.

## Logic

- `buildItems()` (`model.ts`) turns the data into objects; the classification tables (`SERVICE_PHASE_STEPS`, `PIPELINE_STEP`, `RULE_STEP` + `RULE_KIND_STEP`, `TEMPLATE_STEP`, `ASSET_PLACE`) put each on a journey phase and a group, with fallbacks so new data still lands on the desk.
- `layoutDesk(items, perRow)`: per mat, each group is packed first-fit (row-major, 1x1 and 2x1 footprints, no overlaps) into a sub-mat 3 / 4 / 5 / 9 squares wide, sub-mats are shelf-packed into the 9-square mat, mats are laid out in journey order, 5 per row on landscape stages, 3 on squarish ones, 2 on tall phones; every coordinate is a multiple of the 64 px square (plus the half-square gaps).
- Camera = world point at the stage centre + zoom. `toPlane()` / `toScreen()` invert `rotateX` + perspective exactly, so wheel zoom stays under the cursor and drag keeps the grabbed point under the finger even when tilted; Fit iterates on the projected corners.
- During a gesture (wheel, drag, pinch, fly-to) the world's transform is written through a ref and `requestAnimationFrame` (`will-change` on the world only while moving); on commit the zoom becomes CSS `zoom` on the inner layer and the gesture scale resets to 1 in the same frame, so text and edges are rasterised at the size they are seen (transform-only zoom left Chrome's 3D layers blurry past 100 %).
- The desk stays fitted to the stage until the person moves the camera; it refits when the layout reflows (mats per row change).
- Keyboard (stage or anything inside it focused): `+` / `=` zoom in, `-` zoom out, arrows pan, `0` reset, `F` fit. Tab walks the stage, then each mat's label and its objects in reading order; keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`) flies the object into view (a pointer press does not move the camera).
- Activating a mat label fits that mat; activating an object flies to it at a reading zoom (offset beside the drawer) and opens the drawer. A drag or pinch never activates what it started on (the click after a gesture is swallowed).
- Focus / selection ring on the object's top face in brand aqua, width divided by the zoom so it stays 3 px on screen.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: fly-to moves are instant and the tilt change is not animated.
- Geometry: sheet / form / checklist lie flat (1 px edge drawn as a shadow in the sub-mat plane, to keep the 3D layer count low), card 2 px, token 4 px (three stacked discs), document 6 px (page-edge stripes on the sides), folder 8 px (tab carries the iridescent accent), box 18 px (kraft with a tape band); lifted objects get a top face at `translateZ(t)` and front / left / right faces (`backface-visibility: hidden`). One soft shadow per object.
- People (`DeskPerson.tsx`): `layoutDesk(items, perRow, people)` adds a 3-square station row after the sub-mat shelves of every mat that has a person, so a station never shares a square with an object. The station is a `<button>` (320 x 192 world px) after the mat's objects in the Tab order (keyboard focus flies it into view). Layers: a flat rug, the desk (top at `translateZ(22px)` with the wood / paper grain, front / left / right faces), and pop-up planes standing on their bottom edge and leaning back 26° (chair back, the person on the desk's back edge, one standing prop) or 40° (the tent nameplate); flat props are drawn on the desk top. 9 3D layers per station. The figure is inline SVG with a featureless head, one look per portal role (hair shape, outfit colours from brand tokens, skin tones as token mixes). Activating a station flies to it at a zoom where the nameplate reads (beside the drawer) and opens the person drawer. No animation.
- **Open** (object drawer, `desk.openItem`) and **Open portal** (person drawer, `desk.openPersonPortal`): the route is found by exact path, then by pattern (`matchPath`); when `can(route.permission)` is false the session switches to `roleForSurface(route.surface)` (or the first role holding the permission) and toasts "Opening {code} as {role}" (the D-07 wording, D-015), then `navigate(path)`.
- **Light layer: placement** (`deskFlow.ts` `FLOW_RULES`, one rationale per entity): leads -> Lead; engagements -> Brief; meetings -> the current phase (Diagnosis while the project is a lead); documents by kind (contract / brief -> Brief, quote / plan / spec -> Development, project PDF -> Validation, invoice / report -> Delivery, else Brief); quotes and change orders -> Development (supplier quotes are the template's Quotation phase); revision items and brand revisions -> Validation; purchases, payments, deliveries and site reports -> Delivery; messages -> the current phase, on the project's messages sub-mat; alerts -> the current phase. Current phase = the journey phase of `projects.pipelineStatus` (the pipeline token table). Tiles per entity are sorted by their date; at most 6 per entity (the last shows "+N more") and 40 per project (the biggest entity gives way first). Tiles are `DeskItem`s of kind `light` (group `project` / `projectComms`), so `layoutDesk()` packs them like every other object: no overlaps by construction.
- **Money** (rail): Quoted = per `comparisonGroup`, the `selected` quote's `amountCop`, else the lowest `amountCop` of the quotes not `rejected`, summed; Approved = Σ `priceCop` of purchases whose status is not `quoted` + Σ `extraCostCop` of change orders `approved` or `executed`; Paid = Σ `paidCop` of the project's payments (split `direction` in / out); Outstanding = Σ (`amountCop` − `paidCop`) of payments not `paid` (split in / out). Communication = `messages` count in the project channel (the entity has no channel field: it is the official project channel, split client / team by `projects.clientUserId`), `meetings` count, and the lead's `channel`.
- **Trail**: one event per placed row at the entity's own date (lead / document / quote `created_at`, engagement `startedAt`, meeting `startsAt`, change order `approvedAt` else `created_at`, revision item `decidedAt` else `created_at`, brand revision and alert `dueDate`, purchase and site report `date`, payment `paidDate` else `dueDate`, delivery `confirmedDate` else `expectedDate`, message `at`) plus one per `activity` line on the project (field `pipelineStatus`: "Status → <label>", target the status token of the new status) or on one of its rows ("Purchase <ref>: status → Approved"); sorted by time, then entity order. Each event has a bilingual caption, a mat and a target object (its tile, or the last shown tile of its kind when capped). Play advances one stop every 700 ms hop + 650 ms stop and stops at the end (Play again restarts); Previous / Next pause and step; the pulse moves by a CSS `transform` transition (700 ms, straight line at the desk plane, `translateZ(24px)`), instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`; the arrived tile gets `is-lit`. Keys with the stage focused: Space plays / pauses (only when the stage itself has focus, so Space still activates a focused object), `[` / `]` step.
- **Realtime**: the layer is derived from the live lists on every render, so a write in another tab re-renders the tiles, the rail, the drawer fields and the trail. Checked with Playwright (changelog 0035): tab A following Noam, tab B on O-12 ran `ops.advancePurchase {purchase: pur-noam-textiles}`; tab A without reload showed the tile "Approved", Approved COP 67,200,000 -> 71,300,000 and 21 -> 22 events.
- Following or clearing a project reflows the mats and flies to the whole desk once; later reflows keep the camera unless it was never moved.
- Textures: SVG `feTurbulence` noise as data-URI layers over token colours: paper grain, felt fibres on the mats and sub-mats, wood grain (stretched noise) on the desk slab. Light and dark themes set their own materials (`.desk-page` / `.desk-drawer` custom properties).
