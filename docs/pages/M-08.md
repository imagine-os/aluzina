## Purpose

The part of the manual that binds: the fourteen governance rules with the pages that enforce them, the fifteen-status architecture the whole Hub runs on, the validation and purchase statuses, the six playbook roles mapped onto the portals, the eleven operational assets with the templates that already exist, and the ten KPIs. This is the page to open when someone asks "where does the product actually hold us to this?".

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` with breadcrumb Manual > Governance and **Print / PDF**.
2. Rules: one `Card` per `G-01..G-14` — the id, the playbook page, a kind badge (mandatory / commercial / method / gate / principle / change control), the rule, `Enforced by` (the domain's own note) and a row of links to the pages that enforce it, or a `Placeholder` when no page does yet.
3. Status architecture: the 15 `PIPELINE_STATUSES` grouped lead / sale / design / build / close, each as a `StatusPill` with the playbook's own wording (`LEAD - NEW`, `IN CONSTRUCTION`, …).
4. Validation statuses (3) and purchase statuses (6) as pill rows.
5. Roles and responsibilities: the 6 playbook roles, what each one owns, and a link to that role's portal (or "No portal role yet").
6. Operational assets: the 11 assets, what each maps to in the product, and the template status from `deliverables` where one exists.
7. KPI layer: the 10 KPIs with their unit, and a `Placeholder` **Open KPI dashboard**.
8. Final principle as a pull quote.

## Data

Reads `deliverables` (to show the template status of an operational asset). Writes nothing. Everything else is `GOVERNANCE_RULES`, `PIPELINE_STATUSES`, `VALIDATION_STATUSES`, `PURCHASE_STATUSES`, `ROLE_RESPONSIBILITIES`, `OPERATIONAL_ASSETS`, `KPIS`, `FINAL_PRINCIPLE` from `src/domain/playbook.ts`, plus the live route manifest (`useRoutes()`) and `ROLE_META`.

## Rules

All fourteen, rendered — G-01..G-09 mandatory, G-10 commercial, G-11 method, G-12 gate, G-13 principle, G-14 change control. None is enforced here: this page reports where they are enforced.

## Logic

- "Where the product enforces this" is **derived, not hard-coded**: page codes written in a rule's `enforcedBy` are matched against the live routes, and the entity names it mentions (`changeOrders`, `purchases`, `siteReports`, …) are matched against every built page whose `spec.dataTables` declares them (at most three, menu pages only). A page that is removed stops being claimed here on the next render.
- A rule with no page in the product yet shows a `Placeholder` saying so, rather than a dead link.
- Operational assets are matched to `deliverables` rows by exact name through a small UI lookup (`brief-forms` -> "Brief and intake form", and four more); the request to carry the mapping as a column on the entity is in the changelog draft.
- The quick links from M-01 arrive as `?s=<section>`; the page scrolls that section into view and focuses its heading, so the URL addresses the section (P-06).
- `manual.openKpiDashboard` is registered but fails with "not wired yet" — there is no KPI dashboard.

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Real: the rules, the statuses, the roles and their portal links, the assets, the KPIs, the derived enforcing pages, and the deliverable template statuses (from seeded rows). `Placeholder`: **Open KPI dashboard**, plus one per rule that no built page enforces yet (5 on the current route set, including the rules whose enforcement is a status transition rather than a page).

## Responsive and input check (P-01, P-03)

Verified with Playwright at 360 / 390 / 768 / 1280 / 1920 / 2560 / 3840 (`spec.checkedAt`), light and dark, EN and ES: no horizontal page overflow, no console errors. Rule cards are a responsive grid (one column below 768 px); the status groups and the role / asset / KPI rows collapse to one column on phones. Every link and Placeholder is a >= 44 px library `Button` with the global focus ring.

## Strings (P-13)

`manual.governance.*`, `manual.statuses.*`, `manual.kind.*`, `manual.roles.*`, `manual.assets.*`, `manual.kpis.*`; EN and ES complete. Rule text, status labels and KPI names come from `playbook.ts`; the playbook's own status wording (`LEAD - NEW`) is shown untranslated on purpose.

## Open questions

- `GovernanceRule` has no `pageCode`: the derivation from `enforcedBy` works but is a heuristic. Should the domain carry the enforcing page codes explicitly?
- Should a rule card show a live count of the rows that prove it (site reports this month, change orders awaiting approval), or stay a reference page?
