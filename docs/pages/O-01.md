- route: `/#/ops`

- status: built

## Purpose

Miguel's landing page. It answers, in one screen, the five questions his mandate ("ALUZINA's operations organized") asks every morning: what is pending, what is about to become urgent, what arrives this fortnight, who owes us, and whom we owe. Nothing here is a summary of somebody else's page only: the alerts can be acknowledged and the deliveries confirmed without leaving it.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (O-01) with a `Placeholder` "New task" button.
2. Five `StatTile`s, each opening the page that owns the number: pending tasks (hint: high or urgent), open alerts (hint: urgent), deliveries in 14 days (hint: still unconfirmed), clients owe us (hint: overdue count), we owe suppliers (hint: next due date).
3. Card "Next meetings and commitments": `DataTable` of the next five meetings (when, meeting, where), "See all" opens O-02.
4. Card "Pending tasks": the six nearest tasks that are not done; a row opens O-03.
5. Card "Before it becomes urgent": open alerts with severity and days left, each with an Acknowledge row action.
6. Card "Deliveries this fortnight": deliveries expected within 14 days, with a Confirm date row action.
7. Card "Money due in and out": every unpaid payment with direction, outstanding balance and days left; a row opens O-07.

## Data

Reads `tasks`, `meetings`, `alerts`, `deliveries`, `payments`, `projects`, `suppliers`. Writes `alerts.status` (acknowledge) and `deliveries.confirmedDate` / `deliveries.status` (confirm).

## Rules

None numbered yet. The alert lead-time pattern follows `docs/knowledge/roles-and-portals.md` ("Alerts").

## Logic

- Tiles count live rows: tasks not done; alerts `open`; deliveries not delivered with `daysUntil(expectedDate) <= 14`; unpaid payments summed as `amountCop - paidCop`, split by direction.
- Acknowledging writes `alerts.status = 'acknowledged'`; confirming writes `confirmedDate = expectedDate` and `status = 'confirmed'`, the same write O-06 makes.
- Every date column says "due today", "in n days" or "n days overdue" (`dueLabel` in `helpers.ts`).

## Actions (P-05)

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ops.openSection` | Open section | open the {section} section of operations | `projects.read` | `section: string` |
| `ops.acknowledgeAlert` | Acknowledge alert | acknowledge the alert {alert} | `alerts.manage` | `alert: id` |
| `ops.confirmDelivery` | Confirm delivery | confirm the delivery {delivery} for {date} | `deliveries.manage` | `delivery: id`, `date: date` |
| `ops.newTask` | New task | create a task | `tasks.manage` | `title: string` |
