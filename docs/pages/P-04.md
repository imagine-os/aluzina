# P-04 - Our method

- route: `/#/method`
- surface: public (bare shell, no permission)
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/public/specs.ts`
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

The studio's operating logic written for a visitor rather than for the team. It explains why Aluzina can carry a two-hour consultation and a building site with the same method, what the ten steps of a project are, what comprehensive design promises, why design and construction are separate stages, who works on a project, and why the studio works this way. It is the page that makes the ladder on P-01 feel like one studio instead of five products.

## Rules

G-11 and G-13 are published as promises to the client. Their ids, the entities that enforce them and the other twelve rules stay inside the OS.

## Logic

- Roles are published generically: only `playbookRole` is rendered, never the internal `note` (which names staff members). Alejandra Guerra is named because she is the studio's public face.
- Nothing on the page is duplicated copy: every sentence is a `pick()` of a playbook `Text` or a `public.method.*` string.
