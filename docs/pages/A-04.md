# A-04 - Quotes and proposals

- route: `/#/founder/proposals`
- surface: founder
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/founder/specs.ts` (`proposalsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

The three stacks of paper the founder owns: the client documents she writes (quotes, project PDFs, briefs), the graphic proposals Angélica prepares for her signature, and the supplier quotes Miguel collected that she wants on the table before a negotiation.

## Screenshots

`docs/screenshots/A-04/en-390.jpg`, `en-1280.jpg` — captured by the integrator.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (A-04) with a "New client quote" `Placeholder`.
2. `Tabs` with counts: client documents / graphic proposals / supplier quotes.
3. Client documents: `DataTable` (title, kind badge, project, version, status) with row actions "Mark final" and "Send to client".
4. Graphic proposals: `DataTable` of presentations (title, project, owner, slides, due, status) with "Sign off" on the ones in review.
5. Supplier quotes: `DataTable` (comparison group, item, project, amount, lead time, valid until, status) with "Shortlist", and a footer line saying Miguel selects the winner in O-05.

## Data

Reads `documents`, `presentations`, `quotes`, `projects`. Writes `documents.status`, `presentations.status`, `quotes.status`.

## Rules

None numbered. The quote flow follows `roles-and-portals.md` ("Quotes": Miguel requests and compares, the founder writes the client quote).

## Logic

- Client documents = `documents` of kind `quote`, `project-pdf` or `brief`; kinds are shown as badges so the three stacks stay legible in one table.
- Mark final: `draft -> final`. Send to client: `final -> sent`. Both need `proposals.write`.
- Sign off: `presentations.status review -> final` (`proposals.write`), only on proposals and sales decks.
- Shortlist: `quotes.status -> shortlisted` (`quotes.review`), only offered on quotes still `received`.
- Supplier names are not shown: `suppliers` is not in the founder's read set yet (request filed), so the comparison group and item identify the row.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `founder.finaliseDocument` | Mark document final | mark the document {document} as final | `proposals.write` | `document: id` |
| `founder.sendDocument` | Send document to the client | send the document {document} to the client | `proposals.write` | `document: id` |
| `founder.signOffPresentation` | Sign off presentation | sign off the presentation {presentation} | `proposals.write` | `presentation: id` |
| `founder.shortlistQuote` | Shortlist supplier quote | shortlist the supplier quote {quote} | `quotes.review` | `quote: id` |
| `founder.newClientQuote` | New client quote | write a new client quote for {project} | `proposals.write` | `project: id` |
