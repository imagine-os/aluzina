# A-08 - Leads

- route: `/#/founder/leads`
- surface: founder
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/founder/specs.ts` (`leadsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

The single traceable pipeline entry the playbook asks for (p. 3): "every inquiry must enter a single traceable pipeline". Alejandra registers the inquiry with its lead record and commercial data, answers the ten qualification questions, lets the diagnosis suggest a service, decides the service herself, assigns an owner, advances the lead through the four commercial statuses and — at `contracted` — converts it into a project with its engagement. From that point A-03, the Work views and the studio checklists take over.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (A-08) with a "New lead" primary `Button` (only with `leads.manage`).
2. Five `StatTile`s: new, qualified, proposal sent, contracted, and the lead → contract conversion rate.
3. `FilterBar`: `SearchField` (name, city, email, notes), status `Select` over `LEAD_STATUS_IDS`, channel `Select` over `LEAD_CHANNELS`, service `Select` over `SERVICES`, count, Clear.
4. `DataTable` of leads: lead, city, project type, requested service with the suggested one as a second `Badge` when they differ, channel, budget, desired start, owner, status `StatusPill`, source.
5. `Modal` "New lead": the `LEAD_RECORD_FIELDS` block (name, phone, email, city, project type, area, current project status) and the `COMMERCIAL_FIELDS` block (requested service, budget, desired start, channel, owner), plus notes.
6. `Drawer` on row activation: the record as a `KeyValue`; "Service routing" (the G-10 note, "Suggest service", the reason, the requested-service `Select`); "Owner" `Select`; "Lead status" `Select` with ◀ / ▶ buttons; the ten qualification questions (a `Select` where the question has closed answers, a `Textarea` where it does not); notes; "Save qualification and notes"; and, at `contracted`, "Convert to project" plus "Open in Work" once converted.

## Data

Reads `leads`; reads and writes `projects` and `engagements` on conversion. Writes `leads.qualification`, `notes`, `suggestedService`, `requestedService`, `ownerId`, `status`, `projectId`, and creates `leads` rows.

## Rules

- **G-01** every lead has one owner inside ALUZINA (the owner `Select`).
- **G-10** never sell a small service when the diagnosis shows a deeper scope: `routeService()` writes `suggestedService` only; `requestedService` is the founder's decision and is never written by the suggestion.
- **G-03** the contract is documented: the conversion writes the project, the engagement and `leads.projectId` in one flow.

## Logic

- A lead created here is `status: 'lead-new'`, `source: 'manual'`; the public intake form (P-01) will write `source: 'public-intake'`.
- "Suggest service" runs `routeService(answers)` over the draft answers, writes `leads.suggestedService` and shows the reason (`pick(route.reason, lang)`); the action returns `{ service, reason, then }` so voice and WebMCP get the same explanation.
- Status is limited to `LEAD_STATUS_IDS`; the ◀ / ▶ buttons walk the same four, and a call asking for anything else is refused with a readable reason instead of writing.
- **Convert to project** is offered only at `contracted` with `projectId === null`. It re-reads the stored lead first, so a second call (button, another tab, the actions bus) can never create a second project. It creates: `projects` (name and client from the lead, `clientUserId: null`, type — `other` falls back to `residential`, `phase: 'lead'`, `pipelineStatus: 'contracted'`, `serviceCode` = requested ?? suggested ?? `01`, `creativeDirection: 'pending'`, `approval: 'draft'`, `leadDesignerId` = the studio demo user, `budgetCop` from the lead or 0, `startDate` = desired start or today, `location` = city, `summary` = notes), `engagements` (that service, its first phase id, empty `checks` and `brief`, `status: 'started'`), and `leads.projectId`.
- Qualification answers and notes are a local draft saved together by one button. `founder.qualifyLead` also writes one answer directly when given `question` + `answer`, merging it onto the stored row.
- Every update passes `basedOn: row.updated_at` (D-024).

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `founder.newLead` | New lead | register a new lead called {name} | `leads.manage` | `name: string`, `city: string`, `email: string`, `channel: enum`, `projectType: string` |
| `founder.openLead` | Open lead | open the lead {lead} | `leads.read` | `lead: id` |
| `founder.qualifyLead` | Record the qualification | answer {question} with {answer} on the lead {lead} | `leads.manage` | `lead: id`, `question: string`, `answer: string` |
| `founder.suggestService` | Suggest a service | suggest the right service for the lead {lead} | `leads.manage` | `lead: id` |
| `founder.setLeadService` | Set the requested service | set the service of the lead {lead} to {service} | `leads.manage` | `lead: id`, `service: enum:01\|02\|03\|E\|04` |
| `founder.assignLeadOwner` | Assign the owner | assign the lead {lead} to {owner} | `leads.manage` | `lead: id`, `owner: id` |
| `founder.advanceLead` | Advance the lead | move the lead {lead} to {status} | `leads.manage` | `lead: id`, `status: enum` (four lead statuses) |
| `founder.convertLead` | Convert to project | convert the lead {lead} into a project | `leads.manage` | `lead: id` |
| `founder.filterLeads` | Filter leads | show the leads with status {status}, channel {channel} and service {service} | `leads.read` | `status`, `channel`, `service` |
| `founder.searchLeads` | Search leads | search the leads for {query} | `leads.read` | `query: string` |

All ten register while mounted and were run through `window.__aluzina.actions.run` in the browser during the pass.

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Everything on the page is a real write through the provider: creating a lead, the qualification, the routing suggestion, the service and owner decisions, the status walk and the conversion (project + engagement + `projectId`). No `Placeholder` on this page. The data is the seeded eight leads (`src/data/seed/services.ts`), which is mock content, not mock behaviour.

## Responsive and input check (P-01, P-03)

Verified at 360 / 390 / 768 / 1280 / 1920 / 2560 / 3840 with Playwright: no horizontal page scroll at any width, no console errors. The table collapses to stacked cards under 768 and keeps its own scroller above it. Every control is a library control, so all are ≥ 44 px (measured: no focusable element under 44 px tall on this page); the Modal and Drawer trap focus, close on Esc and return focus to the opener. Nothing is hover-only and nothing is drag-only: the status walk is a `Select` plus prev / next buttons.
