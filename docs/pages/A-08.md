## Purpose

The single traceable pipeline entry the playbook asks for (p. 3): "every inquiry must enter a single traceable pipeline". Alejandra registers the inquiry with its lead record and commercial data, answers the ten qualification questions, lets the diagnosis suggest a service, decides the service herself, assigns an owner, advances the lead through the four commercial statuses and — at `contracted` — converts it into a project with its engagement. From that point A-03, the Work views and the studio checklists take over.

## Data

Reads `leads`; reads and writes `projects` and `engagements` on conversion. The desk also reads `messages` (the Notes and messages card: the lead's project channel) and the lead's dossier fields `portraitUrl`, `logoUrl`, `company`, `socials` (D-114; mock leads carry generated placeholder art and fictional `.demo` handles, `SEED_VERSION` 14). Work mats are per person and page in the browser (`storageKey('desk.workmats.A-08')`, ids and squares only; nothing is written into `leads`). Writes `leads.qualification`, `notes`, `suggestedService`, `requestedService`, `ownerId`, `status`, `projectId`, and creates `leads` rows.

## Rules

- **G-01** every lead has one owner inside ALUZINA (the owner `Select`).
- **G-10** never sell a small service when the diagnosis shows a deeper scope: `routeService()` writes `suggestedService` only; `requestedService` is the founder's decision and is never written by the suggestion.
- **G-03** the contract is documented: the conversion writes the project, the engagement and `leads.projectId` in one flow.

## Logic

- A lead created here is `status: 'lead-new'`, `source: 'manual'`; the public intake form (P-01) will write `source: 'public-intake'`.
- "Suggest service" runs `routeService(answers)` over the draft answers, writes `leads.suggestedService` and shows the reason (`pick(route.reason, lang)`); the action returns `{ service, reason, then }` so voice and WebMCP get the same explanation.
- Status is limited to `LEAD_STATUS_IDS`; the ◀ / ▶ buttons walk the same four, and a call asking for anything else is refused with a readable reason instead of writing.
- **Convert to project** is offered only at `contracted` with `projectId === null`. It re-reads the stored lead first, so a second call (button, another tab, the actions bus) can never create a second project. It creates: `projects` (name and client from the lead, `clientUserId: null`, type — `other` falls back to `residential`, `phase: 'lead'`, `pipelineStatus: 'contracted'`, `serviceCode` = requested ?? suggested ?? `01`, `creativeDirection: 'pending'`, `approval: 'draft'`, `leadDesignerId` = the studio demo user, `budgetCop` from the lead or 0, `startDate` = desired start or today, `location` = city, `summary` = notes), `engagements` (that service, its first phase id, empty `checks` and `brief`, `status: 'started'`), and `leads.projectId`.
- Qualification answers and notes are a local draft saved together by one button. `founder.qualifyLead` also writes one answer directly when given `question` + `answer`, merging it onto the stored row.
- Every update passes `basedOn: row.updated_at` (D-024).
- Desk (D-114): selecting a lead card opens its drawer and its dossier as a stack; Fan out lays the cards on an arc that stays inside the Leads mat (at most 8° a card, radius 900 world px, the focused card rises 30 px and comes to the front), Stack collapses them over the lead card (scale 0.42, 1.6 px offsets); both are transform transitions (360 ms, none under reduced motion). Sort: kind (contact, company, socials, qualification, commercial, notes), date (the card's date, undated last), network (social cards by network, then the rest). Flip turns a social card (rotateY 180°, the back is the notes). Several dossiers stay open at once (three fanned measured in changelog 0041).
- Work mats hold references: a lead on a work mat is drawn from its live row (its status pill updates with the process stack), and its process card stays on its status sub-mat. Drag a work-mat card with a mouse, finger or pen (it snaps to squares on release; dropped outside a work mat or on a taken square it goes back with a toast); arrow keys on a focused one move it a square; Move to… and the actions do it without a pointer (P-03). The layout engine places each stored square and moves a conflicting or off-grid one to the first free square, so a work mat never overlaps (checked by `desk:check`).

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Everything on the page is a real write through the provider: creating a lead, the qualification, the routing suggestion, the service and owner decisions, the status walk and the conversion (project + engagement + `projectId`). No `Placeholder` on this page, the desk included (placeholder: none): the dossier, its controls, Copy, Visit (a real link to `example.com` where the mock profile has one; disabled with its reason where it does not) and the work mats all work. The portraits and logos are generated placeholder art (`src/tenant/seed/leads/art.ts`: a featureless figure and a monogram, never a likeness), and the social profiles are drawn from the lead's own data, never embedded from the networks. The data is the seeded eight leads (`src/tenant/seed/services.ts`), which is mock content, not mock behaviour.
