# G-01 - Brand dashboard

- route: `/#/brand`
- surface: brand (Angélica, Graphic Design and Communication)
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/brand/specs.ts` (`homeSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

Angélica opens this first. It answers two questions: what do I owe next, and what is waiting on me. It merges every dated item she owns (competition submission dates, presentation due dates, revision due dates), shows the alerts addressed to her role, and gives one card per section of her portal.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (title, subtitle, breadcrumb).
2. Four `StatTile`s: competitions 2027 (with a hint counting dated vs unknown), open presentations, revisions in the queue, current brand assets. Each navigates.
3. `Card` "Next deadlines": up to eight dated items, soonest first, each with its kind, its status pill and a date pill toned by urgency.
4. `Card` "Alerts for this role": open `alerts` with `forRole = brand`, read-only.
5. Six section `Card`s (competitions, presentations, identity, images, revisions, assets), each a single large button.

## Data

Reads `competitions`, `presentations`, `revisions`, `brandAssets`, `alerts`. Writes nothing.

## Rules

P-01 (widths), P-03 (input), P-05 (actions), P-09 (placeholders), P-13 (strings). The "unknown, never invented" rule of `docs/knowledge/competitions.md`: with no submission dates shared, the competitions tile reads "0 with a date, 20 unknown" and the deadlines card shows only the items that really have dates.

## Logic

- Deadlines merge three entities into one sorted list; final presentations and approved revisions drop out.
- `daysUntil()` sets the date pill: past = `overdue`, within 7 days = `due`, otherwise neutral.
- Alerts are read-only here: `alerts.manage` belongs to Miguel's role, so the page shows no control it cannot honour.
