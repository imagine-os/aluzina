## Purpose

HOY (a wellness studio; project `prj-hoy`, client `cl-hoy`) is an ALUZINA client with its own OS at https://imagine-os.github.io/hoy/. Justin (prompt 0030): "arrange these on mats in aluzina dashboard brilliantly, to make it all feel physical and wow ... with the designated users like we did for aluzina ... each system's point of view can see into the hoy hub contents in its own way". This page lays HOY's whole hub on the desk: one felt mat per HOY role (Customer, Web · Public, Teacher, Front desk, Coordinator, Finance, Admin, Super admin) with a seated figure of that role, the role's experiences as sub-mats, and every one of the 87 pages as a device whose face is the real screen: phones for the member and teacher apps, tall pages and a fanned stack of the whole site for the website, screens on stands for the staff and admin desktops, documents for the manual and docs. Any screen opens in a drawer with what it is for and the actions it declares, and can be opened live, signed in as that role, without leaving ALUZINA.

## Logic

- `useHubMap('hoy')`: snapshot first (lazy chunk), live fetch in parallel with the default HTTP cache, 10-minute stale-while-revalidate in memory; failures keep the snapshot and say why.
- `buildLens(map, lens, ctx)` builds the model; mats in outside-in role order; an experience with several page groups gets one sub-mat per group, label and order from the map (`page.group`, HOY 0.11.1: the member app Book 10 / Pay 9 / Account 13 / Sign in 5, Admin Admin 9 / Content 7 / Tables 1), a page without a group falls back to the local split (member app) or the experience; a group over 16 pages in balanced halves; the On its own lens draws each experience as its device (phones, the website's fanned stack, documents, screens); the website mat starts with a fanned stack of all its pages; maintenance owns no experience and gets no mat; caps 16 per sub-mat / 48 per mat (every page visible).
- Faces: the capture in the faces' language, dark when the app is dark; fallbacks: the other language, the full 390 / 1280 capture, the other device form; website pages use the full-length capture from the top. Offline or before a capture exists: the drawn device with code and title.
- Open live: the map's embed pattern with the object's role (the mat's role when the page allows it), the faces' language and the app theme; iframe only on Open live, removed on Close live or when the drawer closes; real device size scaled to the drawer.
