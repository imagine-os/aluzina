# S-07 - Render and supplier packs

- route: `/#/studio/packs`
- surface: studio
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/studio/specs.ts` (`packsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

The information Sarai prepares for the render artist and for suppliers. A board shows every pack at its stage - briefing, sent, rendering, delivered - with the project it belongs to and how many views it covers.

## Screenshots

Not captured in this pass; working captures at 390 and 1280 in the worker scratchpad.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` with a `Placeholder` "New pack".
2. Three `StatTile`s: in flight, delivered, views briefed.
3. `Kanban` with the four stages; each card shows title, "N views - project" and an audience `Badge`, and carries explicit move buttons.
4. `Drawer`: project, audience, views, due, status, last change; `Placeholder` "Attach pack contents".

## Data

Reads `renderPacks`, `projects`. Writes `renderPacks.status`.

## Rules

None beyond the permission: a pack may move to any stage (packs do come back from the render artist).

## Logic

- `onMove` writes the target column as the pack status and toasts the new stage; it is passed only with `renders.brief`, so a read-only viewer sees a board without move buttons.
- The board uses move buttons instead of drag (P-03), so keyboard, touch, pen and a future d-pad all work.

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `studio.movePack` | Move pack | move the pack {pack} to {status} | `renders.brief` | `pack: id`, `status: enum:briefing\|sent\|rendering\|delivered` |
| `studio.openPackDetail` | Open pack | open the pack {pack} | `renders.brief` | `pack: id` |
| `studio.newPack` | New pack | brief a new pack for {project} | `renders.brief` | `project: id` |
| `studio.attachPackContents` | Attach pack contents | attach the contents of the pack {pack} | `renders.brief` | `pack: id` |

## Components

`PageHeader`, `StatTile`, `Kanban`, `Drawer`, `KeyValue`, `StatusPill`, `Badge`, `Placeholder`, `Button`.

## Real vs mock / placeholder

Real: the board, the stage counts and the move write. `Placeholder`: new pack, attach contents (the pack's drawings, materials and views have no entity yet).

## Responsive and input check (P-01, P-03)

Verified at 360 / 390 / 768 / 1280 / 1920, light and dark. The `Kanban` scrolls horizontally inside itself (`overflow-x: auto`, scroll snap) and does not widen the page; cards and move buttons are >= 44 px.

## Strings (P-13)

Namespace `studio.packs.*` plus shared keys. EN complete, ES complete.
