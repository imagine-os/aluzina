# C-03 - Approvals and revision matrix (client)

- route: `/#/client/approvals`
- surface: client (PhoneShell)
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/client/specs.ts` (`approvalsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

The client side of the single revision matrix (service 03 stage 10, rule G-05) and of the approval gate before execution (G-06). Every comment on the design is a row here, the client adds their own, decides the ones waiting for them, and approves the proposal for execution once nothing is left in revision. This page is the reason the studio can say "not in WhatsApp" and mean it.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` (C-03).
2. Raised `Card`: the rule "All comments are collected here, not in WhatsApp", why (G-05), and a badge with the number waiting for a decision.
3. Per project, `Card` "Approve the proposal for execution": the gate text from the playbook, the current verdict in bold, the gate button (disabled with the reason when it cannot fire) and a `client-approved` pill once it has.
4. Per project, one `Card` per stage of the matrix; each item shows what it is about, its status pill, who wrote it, the comment, the decision date, and — while it is undecided — a `Select` of the three validation statuses with a Confirm button.
5. `Card` "Add a comment": stage `Select` (the phases of the project's service, defaulting to the phase the studio is in), what it is about, the comment, submit.

## Data

Reads `projects` (scoped), `engagements`, `revisionItems`. Writes `revisionItems` (create and update) and `projects.approval`.

## Rules

- **G-05**: design revisions are consolidated into a single revision matrix — a client comment is a `revisionItems` row, never free text on the project.
- **Stage 10 validation statuses**: `approved`, `approved-with-adjustments`, `revision`.
- **G-06 approval gate**: construction may begin only after the client approves the final design.

## Logic

- Items are `revisionItems` filtered to my project ids, grouped by `stage` in the order the comments were written.
- **Add**: creates a row with `source: 'client'`, `status: 'revision'`, `decidedAt: null`, `authorId` = me, `engagementId` = the project's engagement, `stage` = the label of the chosen phase. Empty text is refused with a toast.
- **Decide**: writes `status` and `decidedAt` (today) on that row, with `basedOn` the row the client was looking at (D-024), so a studio edit in parallel raises the conflict notice instead of vanishing.
- **Gate**: enabled only when the project has at least one revision item, none is still in `revision`, and the client has `own.proposals.approve`; then it writes `projects.approval = 'client-approved'`. Otherwise it is disabled and the card says why ("2 comment(s) are still in revision…"). Real logic, not a `Placeholder`.
- Without `own.revisions.write` the forms are not rendered and the write actions are not registered (the page stays a readable matrix).

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `client.addRevisionComment` | Add a comment | add the comment {comment} about {item} at the stage {stage} of {project} | `own.revisions.write` | `project: id`, `stage: string`, `item: string`, `comment: string` |
| `client.decideRevision` | Decide a revision item | mark the revision item {item} as {status} | `own.revisions.write` | `item: id`, `status: enum:approved\|approved-with-adjustments\|revision` |
| `client.approveForExecution` | Approve for execution | approve the proposal of {project} for execution | `own.proposals.approve` | `project: id` |
| `client.selectProject` | Select project | use the project {project} in the comment form | `own.projects.read` | `project: id` |

`client.approveForExecution` refuses through the same rule the button shows: it answers "2 still in revision" instead of writing.

## Real vs mock / placeholder

All real, no `Placeholder`. Verified in the browser on the seeded matrix: adding "Cortinas del estudio" made a fifth row, deciding the three open rows as approved enabled the gate, and the gate wrote `client-approved` on Casa Laureles.

## Responsive and input check (P-01, P-03)

Verified at 360 / 390 / 768 / 1280 / 1920, light and dark, EN and ES. Each decision is a real `<form>` (Select + Confirm) submitted by keyboard; all targets are 44 px; the focus ring is 3 px. Known limitation: while the gate button is `disabled` it is out of the tab order, so the reason is carried by the bold text next to it rather than by the button.

## Strings (P-13)

Namespace `client.approvals.*`. EN complete, ES complete (the rule line reads "Todos los comentarios se recogen aquí, no en WhatsApp.").
