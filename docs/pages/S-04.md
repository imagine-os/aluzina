# S-04 - Material palettes

- route: `/#/studio/materials`
- surface: studio
- status: built
- spec: `apps/hub/src/modules/studio/specs.ts` (`materialsSpec`)
- model: Opus 5

## Purpose

The material palette of each project. Sarai walks every material from proposed to sampled to approved (or rejects it), so only approved materials reach a schedule, a supplier pack or the founder.

## Screenshots

Not captured in this pass; working captures at 390 and 1280 in the worker scratchpad.

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. `PageHeader` with a `Placeholder` "New palette".
2. `FilterBar`: `SearchField` (name, category, finish, colour, palette), project `Select`, status `Select`.
3. One `Card` per palette (count of materials and approved ones as subtitle) with a `DataTable`: name, category, finish, colour, unit price, status, row actions.
4. `Drawer`: project, palette, category, finish, colour, unit price, supplier, status.

## Data

Reads `materials`, `projects`, `suppliers`. Writes `materials.status`.

## Rules

Status ladder: Request sample only from `proposed`; Approve from `sampled` or `rejected`; Reject from anything not already rejected. All three need `materials.manage`.

## Logic

- Palettes are derived from the live rows and sorted by name; a palette disappears when the filters hide all of its materials.
- `unitCop` may be null (product materials not quoted yet): shown as "Unknown", never as zero (D-002 "unknown facts stay unknown").

## Actions (P-05)

| id | label | intent | permission | params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `studio.sampleMaterial` | Request sample | request a sample of the material {material} | `materials.manage` | `material: id` |
| `studio.approveMaterial` | Approve material | approve the material {material} | `materials.manage` | `material: id` |
| `studio.rejectMaterial` | Reject material | reject the material {material} | `materials.manage` | `material: id` |
| `studio.openMaterial` | Open material | open the material {material} | `materials.manage` | `material: id` |
| `studio.newPalette` | New palette | create a material palette for {project} | `materials.manage` | `project: id` |
